# Artifact conservation

Status: Approved

<a id="1"></a>
## 1

The workflow shall retain original source B before the first target change, audited source A, and final source F, including dirty and untracked files.

<a id="2"></a>
## 2

The server shall preserve the original baseline through corrections and shall reject missing or corrupt affected evidence.

<a id="3"></a>
## 3

When design introduces an `ADD`, the server shall require successful native semantic search receipts from B and dispositions for actual returned candidates.

<a id="4"></a>
## 4

The server shall compare exact planned transitions with source-bound observations and shall report unsupported measurements as unknown.

<a id="5"></a>
## 5

Every positive measured increase and new public surface shall require requirement-linked justification reviewed independently of delivery.

<a id="6"></a>
## 6

For affected text, review shall assess resynthesis against the combined requirements and preserve explicitly identified obligations.

<a id="7"></a>
## 7

The server shall manage review lanes, findings, ownership, history, disagreements, source bindings, and acceptance through typed atomic operations.

<a id="8"></a>
## 8

Known mechanical failures shall prevent acceptance and shall not be waived as unsupported checks.

<a id="9"></a>
## 9

The same gate validator shall govern explanatory checks, review acceptance, and coordination mutations that enter implementation or claim completion.

<a id="10"></a>
## 10

Legacy evidence shall remain readable and shall migrate explicitly without fabricating an original baseline or importing historical acceptance.

<a id="11"></a>
## 11

Context-budget FIT shall remain separate from conservation checks.
