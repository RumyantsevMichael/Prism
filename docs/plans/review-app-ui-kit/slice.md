# Review app UI kit

> Shipped history: initiative `review-app-ui-kit` shipped on 2026-11-09, so this document records finished work.
> Current intent lives in Approved requirements, Accepted ADRs, the glossary, and feature files.

The review app moves onto one kit: design values in one file, one `prism-*` custom element per kind of control, theme and density choices stored by the review app, shipped fonts, a served catalog, and checks that refuse styling around the kit.
[mockup/mockup.html](mockup/mockup.html) shows the chosen look in both themes and both densities at 1440, 820, and 390px, built from [mockup/tokens.css](mockup/tokens.css), which holds the design values, and the fonts in [mockup/fonts/](mockup/fonts/).
The architecture is recorded in [own-ui-kit.md](../../ADRs/review-ui-kit/own-ui-kit.md).

The requirements' design questions are answered here.
The IDE-like look is the mockup's: one sidebar on a tinted canvas as the only navigation, each page an island that starts with a page bar, overlays of frosted glass, 24px controls and 22px rows at 13px text, and radii that grow from controls to islands to overlays.
The review app stores the theme and density for the user (clause 16), because browser storage is lost when the port changes.
The shades are the mockup's: every hue of the palette stays, and each shade reaches the pairs of clause 10, which Michael accepted on 8 November 2026.

The mockup shows a few things this contract leaves out: the claim actions `Take the slice`, `Remind`, and `Follow` (removed from the mockup; they are later work), a holder's last report, and people as claim holders, because Prism's claim records do not say whether a person holds a claim (clause 66).

Three obligations of this contract go beyond the Approved requirements and are proposed as requirements 14 to 16 in [requirements-additions.diff](requirements-additions.diff): showing claims (clauses 52, 53, and 66 to 68), naming records by title and kind (clauses 69 and 80), and readable text on glass (clauses 10, 21, 22, and 56); once they are approved, the slices that hold those clauses cite them.

In this contract `<base>` is a project's review URL without its trailing slash, `kit/` and `styles/` are folders of `src/server/review/public/`, and each frozen test names the clauses it checks at the start of its title.

## Contract

### Files and routes

1. The review server serves every file under `kit/` at `<base>/kit/<path>` and every `styles/<name>.css` at `<base>/styles/<name>.css`, typed `text/css; charset=utf-8`, `text/javascript; charset=utf-8`, `text/html; charset=utf-8`, `text/plain; charset=utf-8`, or `font/woff2` by the extensions `.css`, `.js`, `.html`, `.txt`, and `.woff2`, with the same security headers as the review page.
2. A path under `kit/` or `styles/` that does not exist, leaves its folder, or has any other extension answers 404.
3. `<base>/kit/catalog` serves the catalog page.
4. The review page's head loads `kit/boot.js` as a classic script, then `kit/tokens.css`, `kit/kit.css`, and the area stylesheets in that order, and the page loads `kit/kit.js` as a module before `review.js`.
5. The build copies `kit/` and `styles/` to `dist/server/review/public/` byte for byte, as it copies the other browser assets.

### Design values

6. `kit/tokens.css` declares every custom property that [mockup/tokens.css](mockup/tokens.css) declares, with the same value under the same selector and media condition, and the same `@font-face` rules, and no other file of the review app declares a raw design value.
7. A theme is one rule that gives a value to every semantic token, which are the `--prism-color-*` and `--prism-shadow-*` properties and `--prism-gradient-canvas`, under `[data-theme="<name>"]`: the shipped themes `light` and `dark` are two such rules that declare the same set of names, the light rule's selector list is exactly `:root, [data-theme="light"]` so light is the default, no other rule of `kit/tokens.css` declares a semantic token, compact sizes sit on `:root` and comfortable sizes on `[data-density="comfortable"]`, and a theme rule may also restate `--prism-island-gap`, `--prism-radius-island`, `--prism-radius-overlay`, `--prism-blur-glass`, `--prism-saturate-glass`, and `--prism-blur-scrim`, so a theme can flatten the islands, while any element that carries one of these attributes renders its subtree in that theme or density.
8. Every color the review page and the catalog paint, including code highlighting, diff lines, status tones, phases, claims, the brand mark's tint, and diagrams, comes from a semantic token, and shadow-DOM controls read the same tokens through inheritance, so a rule `[data-theme="<other>"]` that gives the dark theme's values makes every element compute exactly the colors it computes in the dark theme; custom themes beyond light and dark are not built now.
9. Inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` every `--prism-duration-*` property is `0ms`, and every transition and animation of the review page and the catalog takes its duration from a `--prism-duration-*` property, so all computed transition and animation durations are zero there.
10. In each theme, where a name below stands for `--prism-color-<name>`: `ink`, `ink-muted`, and `accent-text` reach 4.5:1 and `line-control` and `focus` reach 3:1 on `bg`, `chrome`, `chrome-hover`, `surface`, `sunken`, `raised`, `hover`, and `selected`; `danger`, `warning`, `success`, and `info` reach 4.5:1 on `surface`, `raised`, `sunken`, and their own `-soft` color; `ink` reaches 4.5:1 on every `-soft` color; `accent-text` reaches 4.5:1 on `accent-soft` and `on-accent` on `accent`; `diff-added` and `diff-removed` reach 4.5:1 on `surface`, `sunken`, and their own `-soft` color; every `syntax-*` color reaches 4.5:1 on `surface` and `sunken`; each `phase-<phase>` reaches 4.5:1 and each `phase-<phase>-fill` 3:1 on `bg`, `surface`, `sunken`, and `raised`; `on-agent` reaches 4.5:1 on `agent` and each `on-person-<n>` on `person-<n>`; `live` reaches 3:1 on `bg`, `surface`, and `chrome`; composed over pure black and over pure white, `glass` keeps `ink`, `ink-muted`, and `accent-text` at 4.5:1 and `glass-control` and `focus` at 3:1; and `diagram-ink` reaches 4.5:1 on `diagram-paper`, `diagram-surface`, `diagram-surface-2`, `diagram-group`, and `diagram-note`, and `diagram-line`, `diagram-note-line`, and `diagram-danger` 3:1 on `diagram-paper`; the guarantee covers the shipped themes only.

### Fonts

11. `kit/fonts/` holds Geist and Geist Mono as the variable WOFF2 files `geist-latin-wght-normal.woff2` and `geist-mono-latin-wght-normal.woff2` with their SIL Open Font License texts `OFL-Geist.txt` and `OFL-Geist-Mono.txt`, and `kit/tokens.css` declares them as the font families `Prism UI` and `Prism Code` with sources relative to itself.
12. `--prism-font-ui` starts with `"Prism UI"` and `--prism-font-code` with `"Prism Code"`, and the review page renders body text in Prism UI and `code`, `pre`, and `kbd` text in Prism Code, both loaded from the review app while every other host is unreachable.

### Preferences

13. `GET <base>/api/preferences` answers 200 with `{"theme":"system"|"light"|"dark","density":"compact"|"comfortable"}`, giving `system` and `compact` for a value that is missing, unreadable, or invalid in storage.
14. `PUT <base>/api/preferences` with a JSON object holding `theme`, `density`, or both stores those values, keeps the stored value of the key it leaves out, and answers 200 with the full stored preferences.
15. A PUT whose body is not a JSON object, holds neither key, holds another key, or gives another value answers 400 with `{"code":"invalid_preference","message":<text>}` and stores nothing, and a PUT without a JSON content type or with an `Origin` of another host answers 403.
16. The review app stores the preferences in `review-preferences.json` in the review app folder, which is `PRISM_REVIEW_APP_DIR` when set and the platform's Prism state folder otherwise, so every project, port, and private session server of one user reads the same choice, including after a restart on another port.
17. The page sets `data-theme-choice` (`system`, `light`, or `dark`), `data-theme` (`light` or `dark`), and `data-density` (`compact` or `comfortable`) on `<html>`: before the first paint from the last choice this browser saw at this origin, then from the server's answer, which wins, and it keeps the browser's choice or the defaults when the server cannot be reached.
18. While the choice is `system`, `data-theme` follows the operating system's color scheme and changes when that setting changes, without a reload, and while the choice is `light` or `dark` it ignores the operating system.
19. `<meta name="theme-color">` carries the current value of `--prism-color-chrome` and changes with the theme.

### Theme, contrast, and density on every screen

20. Every visible element of the review page and the catalog that paints a non-transparent background color paints a different color in the dark theme than in the light theme, except SVG content.
21. Every visible text of the review page and the catalog reaches 4.5:1 against the background behind it, or 3:1 at 24px and above or at 18.66px bold and above, in both themes and both densities, where the background of text on a translucent surface with a backdrop filter is that surface composed over pure black and over pure white and the text must reach the ratio over both, and text of a disabled control is exempt.
22. Every visible enabled field (a text, search, or select control or a textarea) and choice control (a radio, a checkbox, or a switch, or the indicator drawn for a visually hidden one, marked `data-indicator`) draws a border side or a fill at 3:1 against what surrounds it, in both themes, composed over pure black and over pure white when it sits on glass, while buttons, cards, rows, chips, and avatars rely on the contrast of their text.
23. Every focusable kit control shows a 2px solid outline in `--prism-color-focus` with a 1px offset when it takes focus from the keyboard, in both themes.
24. Compact is the default density, and comfortable raises `--prism-control-height` from 24px to 30px, `--prism-row-height` from 22px to 28px, `--prism-font-size-md` from 13px to 14px, `--prism-island-gap` from 6px to 8px, and every `--prism-space-*` step, while every kit control takes its height from `--prism-control-height`, `--prism-control-height-sm`, `--prism-row-height`, or `--prism-subtab-height`.

### Appearance menu

25. The status bar ends with a kit button named `Appearance` that opens a kit menu holding a `Theme` group of radio items `System`, `Light`, and `Dark`, a `Density` group of radio items `Compact` and `Comfortable`, and an item `Component catalog`, with the current choices checked.
26. Choosing a theme or density item applies it at once, stores it through `PUT <base>/api/preferences`, and closes the menu, and choosing `Component catalog` opens `<base>/kit/catalog` in a new tab.

### Kit elements

27. `kit/kit.js` is an ES module that imports without a DOM and exports `KIT_ELEMENTS`, an array of element classes that each carry a static `tag` and a static `catalog` of `{ purpose, options: [{ name, values, default, description }], states: [<state name>], keyboard: [<sentence>] }`, and `defineKit()`, which defines each element once and does nothing when called again; loading it in a page defines every element.
28. `KIT_ELEMENTS` holds at least these tags, the first eleven rendering in shadow DOM and the rest in light DOM: `prism-button`, `prism-icon`, `prism-chip`, `prism-badge`, `prism-tab`, `prism-menu-item`, `prism-tooltip`, `prism-stat`, `prism-meter`, `prism-spinner`, `prism-avatar`, `prism-tabs`, `prism-menu`, `prism-menu-group`, `prism-field`, `prism-option`, `prism-choice`, `prism-choice-option`, `prism-panel`, `prism-tree`, `prism-tree-item`, `prism-listbox`, `prism-list-item`, `prism-dialog`, `prism-popover`, `prism-disclosure`, `prism-empty-state`, `prism-banner`, `prism-toast`, `prism-claim`, `prism-stepper`, `prism-step`, `prism-breadcrumbs`, `prism-doc`, and `prism-code`.
29. `prism-button` takes `variant` (`default`, `primary`, `ghost`, or `danger`), `size` (`md` or `sm`), `disabled`, `pressed` (exposed as `aria-pressed`), and `label` (its accessible name when it shows only an icon), renders one native button around its content, lets a click of an enabled button reach listeners on the element, sends no click while disabled, and activates on Enter and Space.
30. `prism-icon` takes `name`, one of at least `search`, `close`, `chevron-right`, `chevron-down`, `back`, `forward`, `menu`, `panel`, `link`, `agent`, `store`, `sync`, `maximize`, `restore`, `external`, `check`, `warning`, `info`, and `more`, draws it in the current text color, and stays hidden from assistive technology unless `label` names it.
31. `prism-chip` takes `tone` (`neutral`, `accent`, `danger`, `warning`, `success`, or `info`), `href`, which makes it a link, and `interactive`, which makes it a button, and shows children with `slot="meta"` as quieter secondary text; a chip without either is static text.
32. `prism-badge` takes `tone` as `prism-chip` does and `variant` (`count` or `status`), and is static text with no role of a control.
33. `prism-tabs` has the role tablist named by `label`, and each `prism-tab` has the role tab and takes `value` and `selected` (exposed as `aria-selected`), with exactly one selected tab.
34. In `prism-tabs` only one tab is in the Tab order, ArrowLeft and ArrowRight move focus with wrapping, Home and End move to the first and last tab, Enter, Space, and a click select a tab and send `prism-select` with `detail.value` from the tabs element, and tabs that do not fit scroll inside the strip.
35. `prism-menu` takes `for`, the id of the kit button that opens it, and `open`, sets `aria-haspopup="menu"` and `aria-expanded` on that button, and holds `prism-menu-group` elements named by `label` and `prism-menu-item` elements that take `value`, `type` (`action`, `radio`, or `checkbox`), `checked`, and `disabled`, with the roles menu, group, menuitem, menuitemradio, and menuitemcheckbox and `aria-checked`.
36. Enter, Space, or ArrowDown on a menu's button opens the menu with focus on its first enabled item and ArrowUp with focus on its last, ArrowUp, ArrowDown, Home, and End move focus, Enter, Space, or a click on an item send `prism-select` with `detail.value` from the menu, close it, and return focus to its button, Escape closes it and returns focus, Tab or a click outside closes it, and the open menu stays inside the window, opening above its button when there is no room below.
37. The kit adds one `prism-tooltip` to the document, which shows the full text of a kit element that has a `tip` attribute or whose text is shortened, as an element with the role tooltip that the element names through `aria-describedby`, after `--prism-tooltip-delay` of hover and at once when the element takes keyboard focus, and hides it on pointer leave, blur, or Escape, while kit elements never render a native `title` tooltip.
38. A kit element that shows one line of text ends text that does not fit with an ellipsis, carries the attribute `shortened` while it does, and joins the Tab order while shortened when it is not focusable already, and a kit element that holds more items than fit scrolls them inside itself or wraps them, so no kit element grows past its container.
39. `prism-field` takes `type` (`text`, `search`, `textarea`, or `select`), `label`, which it shows and associates with the control, `hint`, `name`, `value`, `placeholder`, `required`, `disabled`, `invalid`, and `error`, which it shows and links through `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby`, creates one native control in its light DOM, reads and writes that control through its `value` property, lets the control's `input` and `change` events bubble, and turns `prism-option` children with `value` and `selected` into the options of a select.
40. `prism-choice` is a radio group named by `label` that takes `name` and `value` and holds `prism-choice-option` elements that take `value`, `tone`, and `disabled` and show their content with children of `slot="detail"` as a description, creating one native radio input per option, drawn by the input itself or by an indicator marked `data-indicator`, so arrow keys move the choice and a `change` event bubbles after the `value` property updates.
41. `prism-panel` takes `heading`, `level` (2 by default), `eyebrow`, `variant` (`card`, `plain`, `inset`, or `island`), and `maximizable`, which adds a button named `Maximize` or `Restore` that toggles the `maximized` attribute and sends `prism-maximize` with `detail.maximized`, renders its heading as a heading element of that level, places children with `slot="tools"` in its header and `slot="footer"` after its body, and keeps every other child in its body, and an `island` panel takes its corner radius from `--prism-radius-island`, its shadow from `--prism-shadow-island`, and its edge from `--prism-color-island-edge`.
42. `prism-tree` has the role tree named by `label`, and each `prism-tree-item` has the role treeitem with `aria-level`, takes `label`, `value`, `expanded`, and `selected`, shows children with `slot="end"` after its label, holds its child items in an element with the role group, and exposes `aria-expanded` when it has child items.
43. In `prism-tree` only one item is in the Tab order, ArrowDown and ArrowUp move to the next and previous visible item, ArrowRight expands a collapsed item or moves to its first child, ArrowLeft collapses an expanded item or moves to its parent, Home and End move to the first and last visible item, and Enter or a click selects an item and sends `prism-select` with `detail.value` from the tree.
44. `prism-listbox` has the role listbox named by `label` and holds `prism-list-item` options that take `value` and `selected` and show children with `slot="meta"` as their kind; with `for` naming a kit field, focus stays in the field, ArrowDown and ArrowUp move the active option through `aria-activedescendant`, and Enter sends `prism-select` with `detail.value`, and without `for` the listbox itself takes focus and the same keys apply.
45. `prism-dialog` takes `heading`, `open`, `size` (`sm`, `md`, or `lg`), and `placement` (`center`, `start`, or `bottom`), has the role dialog with `aria-modal="true"` named by its heading, opens with `show()` as a modal over a scrim that paints `--prism-color-scrim` and blurs the page behind by `--prism-blur-scrim`, with focus moved inside it and kept inside while open, closes with `close()`, Escape, a click on the scrim, or its close button named `Close`, sends `prism-close`, and returns focus to the element that had it before it opened, where `start` slides in from the left edge at `--prism-drawer-width` and `bottom` rises from the bottom edge at full width.
46. `prism-popover` takes `for`, the id of the kit button that opens it, `heading`, and `open`, sets `aria-haspopup="dialog"` and `aria-expanded` on that button, has the role dialog without `aria-modal` named by its heading, toggles when its button is activated, closes with Escape, a click outside, or focus leaving it, returns focus to its button when Escape closes it, stays inside the window, and spans the window's width at 720px and below.
47. `prism-disclosure` takes `summary` and `open`, shows a toggle button with `aria-expanded` that Enter, Space, and a click toggle, and sends `toggle` when it opens or closes.
48. `prism-empty-state` takes `heading` and shows its children below it, and `prism-banner` takes `tone` (`info`, `warning`, `danger`, or `success`), `heading`, and `dismissible`, which adds a button named `Dismiss` that sends `prism-dismiss`, with the role alert for `danger` and status otherwise.
49. `prism-toast` is one live region with the role status and `aria-live="polite"` whose `show(message, { tone })` replaces any shown message and hides it after a time the kit fixes, at least four seconds and the same under reduced motion.
50. `prism-stat` takes `label`, `value`, `hint`, and `tone`, `prism-meter` takes `value` from 0 to 100, `label`, and `tone`, and has the role progressbar with `aria-valuenow`, or no `aria-valuenow` while it has no value, at `--prism-meter-height`, and `prism-spinner` takes `label`, has the role status, and stands still under reduced motion.
51. `prism-doc` holds rendered Markdown in light DOM and styles its headings, paragraphs, lists, links, tables, quotes, inline code, code blocks, and images from the design values, and `prism-code` holds a code block in light DOM, takes `language`, `numbers`, and `start`, shows line numbers from `start` when `numbers` is set, and colors `tok-<name>` spans with `--prism-color-syntax-<name>`, falling back to `--prism-color-ink` for a name the theme does not define.
52. `prism-avatar` takes `kind` (`agent` or `person`), `name`, `size` (`sm`, `md`, or `lg`, from `--prism-avatar-size-sm`, `--prism-avatar-size`, and `--prism-avatar-size-lg`), `hue` (1 to 4, for a person), `live`, `breathing`, and `expired`: an agent is a square with `--prism-radius-sm` corners that shows the `agent` icon in `--prism-color-on-agent` on `--prism-color-agent`, a person is a circle that shows the first letters of the first two words of `name` in `--prism-color-on-person-<hue>` on `--prism-color-person-<hue>`, its accessible name is `name` followed by ` (agent)` for an agent, `live` adds a dot in `--prism-color-live`, `breathing` makes that dot pulse over `--prism-duration-pulse`, and `expired` turns the avatar hollow with a dashed outline and no dot.
53. `prism-claim` takes `label`, `activity` (`design` or `implementation`), `claimed-at`, `renewed-at`, `expires-at`, and `variant` (`avatar`, `inline`, or `strip`), and shows an agent avatar named `label`, the holder's label followed by the text `Agent`, the activity in words with a facet in `--prism-color-phase-design` for design and `--prism-color-phase-build` for implementation, and the time left in words (`<n> min left` in whole minutes rounded up below an hour, then `<n> h left` in rounded hours below 48 hours, then `<n> days left`); a claim whose `expires-at` has passed shows `Expired <n> min ago` in whole minutes rounded down (or `h`, or `days`, as above) in `--prism-color-warning` with an expired avatar; `avatar` shows only the avatar with the full text as its `tip` and accessible description, `strip` adds `since <time>` and a `prism-meter` whose value is the time left out of `expires-at` minus `renewed-at` in whole percent, the text updates every minute without a reload, and the element holds no control.
54. `prism-stepper` has the role list named by `label` and holds `prism-step` items with the role listitem that take `phase` (`requirements`, `design`, `frozen`, `build`, `review`, or `shipped`), `state` (`done`, `current`, or `upcoming`), and a label, draw a bar at `--prism-meter-height` in `--prism-color-phase-<phase>-fill` for a done or current step and `--prism-color-line-strong` for an upcoming one, write the state in words for assistive technology, and mark the current step with `aria-current="step"`.
55. `prism-breadcrumbs` is a navigation landmark named `Breadcrumb` that turns its child links into an ordered list, marks the last with `aria-current="page"`, and shortens each crumb that does not fit as clause 38 states while keeping the last crumb whole as long as it fits.
56. Menus, popovers, tooltips, dialogs, and the toast paint `--prism-color-glass` with a backdrop filter of `blur(var(--prism-blur-glass)) saturate(var(--prism-saturate-glass))`, an edge in `--prism-color-glass-edge`, corners of `--prism-radius-overlay`, and the shadow `--prism-shadow-glass`, and a field or choice control on glass draws its boundary in `--prism-color-glass-control`.

### Catalog

57. The catalog renders one `<section data-catalog-entry="<tag>">` per element of `KIT_ELEMENTS`, which holds the tag in its heading, `[data-doc="purpose"]`, `[data-doc="options"]`, `[data-doc="states"]`, and `[data-doc="keyboard"]` filled from the element's `catalog`, one `[data-specimen="<state>"]` with a live instance per listed state, and specimens `long-text` and `unbroken-name`, plus `many-items` for an element that holds items.
58. The catalog's first entry, `<section data-catalog-entry="design-values">`, lists every semantic token by name with its value and a swatch in each shipped theme, and every other design value by name and value, read from the stylesheet the page loaded, so the token names are a documented, stable contract.
59. The catalog's toolbar holds kit controls that set its theme (`System`, `Light`, or `Dark`), density, and width (`Narrow` at 320px or `Wide`) for the catalog alone, the query `?theme=light|dark&density=compact|comfortable&width=narrow|wide` sets them on load, and without them the catalog follows the stored preferences.

### Layout check

60. `scripts/review-demo/layout-audit.mjs` exports `checkCatalog({ url, viewports, themes })`, which opens a catalog URL with an installed Playwright at each named viewport (`phone`, `tablet`, `laptop`, `short`, `wide`, or `ultra`) and theme (`light` or `dark`), emulating the theme as the operating system's color scheme and setting it in the `theme` query, and resolves to one `{ entry, viewport, width, theme, problem }` for each catalog entry that escapes its container, cuts off a control, or scrolls the page horizontally, where `entry` is the entry's `data-catalog-entry` and `width` the viewport width in pixels.
61. `scripts/review-demo/layout-check.mjs` also checks the catalog at each of its viewports and themes, or with `--catalog <url>` only the catalog at that URL, prints each catalog problem as `catalog <entry>: <problem> at <viewport> (<width>px), <theme>`, and exits 1 when there is one.
62. Once every slice is done, `node scripts/review-demo/layout-check.mjs --viewports phone,laptop --themes light,dark` finds no problem on any page and overlay of the demo, edge-case, and empty projects or in the catalog, and prints `0 distinct findings`.

### Diagrams

63. `kit/plantuml-defaults.js` exports `PLANTUML_RUNTIME`, the vendored runtime's version `1.2026.8`, and `PLANTUML_DEFAULTS`, which maps each color, as upper-case hex, that the vendored runtime paints when a diagram sets none to a diagram role (`surface`, `surface-2`, `group`, `group-line`, `note`, `line`, or `danger`) or to `null` for a color it keeps, and the colors the vendored `renderToString` paints as `fill` or `stroke` for the reference diagrams of `test/server/fixtures/plantuml-reference.mjs` are exactly the keys of `PLANTUML_DEFAULTS`, so a runtime whose defaults change fails that check.
64. `kit/diagram-theme.js` exports `themeDiagram(svg)`, which recolors an SVG attached to the page from the `--prism-color-diagram-*` values computed on it: each default fill and stroke takes its role's token, the border of a note takes `diagram-note-line`, default text takes `diagram-ink` on a ground the runtime chose or on the paper and stays black on an author's fill that it reads on at 4.5:1, or takes `diagram-ink` otherwise, every color the author set is kept, an author's text color on a default ground or on the paper is lightened or darkened with its hue kept until it reaches 4.5:1 there, and an author's line color until it reaches 3:1 on `diagram-paper`.
65. Every PlantUML diagram the review page shows sits on a sheet painted `--prism-color-diagram-paper` and is recolored by `themeDiagram` once it is drawn and again when the theme changes, starting each time from the SVG text the runtime returned, so no default color that `PLANTUML_DEFAULTS` maps to a role remains in a shown diagram.

### Claims

66. The review page reads claims only from the data the server already returns, the `claim` and `expiredClaim` of each slice in an initiative's record and a slice's `coveringClaim`, shows every holder as an agent with the label the claim records, and offers no control that takes, releases, renews, reminds about, or follows a claim.

## Assumptions

- Probed: the page's policy `style-src 'self'` refuses a `<style>` element in a shadow root but applies an adopted constructed stylesheet and a shadow `<link rel="stylesheet">` from the same origin (a scratch page served with the review server's policy header and opened in Chromium through Playwright 1.63; observed the computed colors of the adopted and linked sheets and a refusal message for the style element).
- Probed: a WOFF2 font from the same origin loads under the page's policy (`document.fonts` reported the face `loaded` in the same probe).
- Probed: browser storage is separate per port (a value stored on one port read as missing on a second port of 127.0.0.1), which is why the review app stores the preferences.
- Probed: Playwright's `emulateMedia({ colorScheme })` fires a `change` event on `matchMedia("(prefers-color-scheme: dark)")`, and a custom property overridden in `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` computes as `0ms` under `emulateMedia({ reducedMotion: "reduce" })`.
- Probed: the values in [mockup/tokens.css](mockup/tokens.css) pass every pair of clause 10 in both themes (320 pairs computed from the file; the light `diagram-note-line` was darkened from the direction's `#b9a45a`, 2.46:1 on the paper, to `#a69147`, 3.11:1, with its hue kept).
- Probed: no `--prism-color-*` value of [mockup/tokens.css](mockup/tokens.css) is the same in the light and the dark theme, which clause 20 needs wherever a token paints a background (the dark `phase-design-fill` and `phase-frozen-fill` were changed from the light `#4f8bff` and `#7a5cff` to the dark phase inks `#6f9fff`, 7.18:1 on `bg`, and `#9a85ff`, 6.37:1, with their hues kept; the spectrum gradient is the brand mark, fixed across themes, and paints no background color).
- Unprobed here, reported by the mockup's designer: the mockup audited at 0 problems at 1440, 820, and 390px in both themes and both densities for layout, text contrast with glass measured over pure black and pure white, and field and choice boundaries, and a copy with the glass fill at 72% failed the glass check; the frozen tests repeat these checks on the app itself.
