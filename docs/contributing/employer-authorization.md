# Employer authorization for a contribution to Prism

A contributor whose employer or client may own their work asks that employer or client to sign this letter before a non-trivial pull request can be merged.
[CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) says when the letter is needed.
Fill in the bracketed fields, have someone who can sign for the company sign it, and send it as a PDF to [hello@unhumankind.com](mailto:hello@unhumankind.com) with the pull request's link.
The maintainer keeps the letter and does not publish it.

---
